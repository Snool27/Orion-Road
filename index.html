Mastery

How the % is calculated

Two ways to read your progress; tap the line under the big number to switch.

By effort (default)

Every step is weighted by roughly how long it takes, so maxing a gun counts far more than one camo tick, and the mastery camos count for the weeks they actually take. Estimates per gun: Military 2.5 h, each Special 0.6 h, Gold 0.4 h, Platinum 1 h (1.5 h for shotguns, LMGs, marksman, snipers, knife; 3 h for launchers), Harmonic Break 1.2 h (2 h; 5 h for launchers). Mastery: Mercurial Drift 40 h, Polyatomic 35 h, Orion 45 h; Chiral 20 h, Ripple Tide 25 h, Helio 100 h; Warzone ones 30–45 h until Season 1 says otherwise. Your % = effort done ÷ effort in your goal.

By count

Plain camo ticks: 14 per gun (12 for a Gold goal, 13 for Platinum) across 33 launch guns (Season 1 guns are optional extras), with mastery counted as a separate 40 % block when a mastery goal is on.

Hour estimates are based on Boosting Market's launch-week timings for the ladder (150–250 h to Divergence) and my own for mastery; they're for pacing, not a stopwatch.

Saving & other devices

Everything you tap saves to your account the moment you tap it (the account is created for you automatically — no email, no password). Tap your name at the top right for your friend code and your private sync key; the sync key is how you become you on another device. Add the page to your home screen to use it like an app, and download a backup now and then anyway.
